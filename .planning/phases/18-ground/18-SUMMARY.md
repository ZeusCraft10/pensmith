# Phase 18: Grounded Generation (GROUND): Summary

## What shipped

Every generative step now gets the real assignment, the intake answers, the topic, the outline entry, the voice and the section's own sources. Every model output is checked against a contract that the next step reads. Bare `pensmith` goes from a folder holding only `assignment.txt` to an exported paper with no hand edits, and `--dry-run` runs the whole workflow in a scratch workspace with zero egress. The Phase 17 carry-over RUN-26 (prompt caching) is closed as well.

Four streams built the phase in parallel from seam S-A (D-18-01, D-18-02). After an integration pass, three adversarial review rounds followed. Round 1 had 29 findings: 27 were fixed in full, and 2 were fixed in part with the other half rejected. Round 2 fixed all 13 of its findings, and round 3 fixed 13 of 14. Every rejected item is listed below with its reason. The per-stream detail is in `18-{intake,sections,llm,workflow}-SUMMARY.md`, and the decisions are D-18-01..47 in `18-CONTEXT.md`.

### Prompt layout, fencing and caching (llm stream; RUN-26, FEED-05, D-18-03..06)
- **Data last, sent once.** All 14 `templates/prompts/*.md` are fixed instruction text with no `{{…}}` slot. Each one's frontmatter has `inputs: [...]`, and a `## Inputs` section describes each tag. `bin/lib/prompt-request.ts` `buildPromptRequest(slug, values)` builds every request: the system prompt is the unmodified template, and the user message is one set of `<tag>…</tag>` blocks in `PROMPT_INPUTS` order, with JSON built field by field. `interpolate()` and `escapeTemplateTokens()` are removed. All 14 templates are re-pinned in `EXPECTED_PROMPT_HASHES` and `tests/repo-files.test.ts`.
- **Caching (RUN-26).** Every slug sends its system prompt as one `cache_control: {type: "ephemeral"}` block on the Anthropic shape, and as the first message on the chat shape. `llm-models.ts` records each model's minimum cacheable prefix: claude-opus-5 and the fable models 512, sonnet-5 and opus-4-8 1024, opus-4-7 2048, haiku-4-5 and opus-4-6 4096, unlisted models reported against 4096. `status --config` gains a `cache yes/no/n/a` column that shows the estimated tokens against the model's minimum. The RUN-21 mock simulates caching (breakpoints, per-model minimums, 5-minute TTL, OpenAI `cached_tokens`). SESSION.log records `cache_read_tokens`, and COSTS prices cache reads at 0.1× input. `--estimate` stays undiscounted, which keeps it conservative. Generation templates reach claude-opus-5's minimum at about 780–1550 tokens. Judgment templates on Haiku 4.5 fall below its 4096 minimum, `status --config` reports that, and no template is padded to reach it.
- **One fence (FEED-05, D-18-04).** `bin/lib/untrusted-fence.ts` is the only place the `PENSMITH_UNTRUSTED_DATA` markers exist; this is a grep test and chokepoint row `untrusted-fence`. The renderer wraps every `untrusted` input and neutralises every spelling of a fence marker and every closing `</tag>`. The marker strip runs in linear time (review round 2 ReDoS fix). The private copies in `pass2.ts` and `pass4.ts` are gone.
- **Contract stubs (GRND-19 stub half, D-18-06).** `PENSMITH_NO_LLM`, `--dry-run` and the mock default all build their replies from the request blocks: structured stubs through `llm-stubs.ts`, text stubs through `bin/lib/llm-text-stubs.ts`, whose prose lives in `templates/stubs/text-stubs.json`. The drafter stub writes to the word target and cites every assigned key. The `[PENSMITH_NO_LLM placeholder — …]` string is gone.

### Intake (intake stream; GRND-01..06, D-18-07..13, D-18-44, D-18-47)
- **Assignment sources (GRND-01).** `--from`, `@path` (.txt/.md/.pdf, with PDFs read through `pdf-text.ts`), piped stdin (2 s first-byte timeout, 1 MiB cap, never read in numbered mode), a cwd `assignment.*` found through the `assignment-pickup` gate, and a multi-line paste in a terminal. A missing file, an unsupported type or empty input fails with one line. With no assignment and no way to prompt, `new` exits 2 and writes nothing.
- **The §7.1 battery (GRND-02).** `bin/lib/intake-questions.ts` holds the 9 questions; the tutorial.ts question is composed without being named. Answers come from flags, `--answers <file.toml>`, @clack/prompts in a terminal or numbered mode. `--yolo` accepts the suggested defaults and prints them. A non-TTY run with unanswered questions exits 3 through the `intake-defaults` gate before any model call. The clarifier (v2 contract) only suggests values and asks at most 3 follow-ups, and its reply is never persisted. Review round 1 fixed TTY defaults (`defaultValue`, never `initialValue`), and `tests/intake-tty.test.ts` drives the real CLI through clack with a fake TTY. `[project] mode = "outline"` stops routing after the approved outline (D-18-45).
- **The brief (GRND-03).** INTAKE.md has v1 frontmatter (`IntakeBriefSchema`, with a v0→v1 migration) and a body with the verbatim assignment and a Q/A section. `config.toml [project]` mirrors the answers. Research seeds its queries from the structured topic.
- **Overrides (GRND-04).** `bin/lib/intake-overrides.ts` handles plain-English style instructions, the alias table for the 8 styles and sectioning notes. The notes reach the outline only in canonical form (D-18-47).
- **PII (GRND-05).** `pii.ts` now matches middle initials, hyphens, particles, labelled IDs, textual dates, spaced SSNs and international phone numbers. It never redacts entity or topic phrases, and names in a person context are always redacted (D-18-44). Identifiers are passed through unchanged, including UUIDs, DOIs, ISBNs, arXiv ids and ISO timestamps (10 000-UUID property test). With redaction on, no model request in new → research → outline → plan → write carries the raw PII.
- **One preset loader (GRND-06).** `bin/lib/disciplines.ts` holds the PRD §8 values plus sociology, with precedence preset < intake < config < flag. `citations.ts` and `citation-density.ts` read styles and per-paragraph density bands through it, and the `discipline-literals` chokepoint row enforces that.

### Outline, plan and write (sections stream; FEED-01..04, GRND-07..10, 12, 13, 15, 16, D-18-14..27, D-18-37..40, D-18-42..43, D-18-46)
- **One outline contract (GRND-07).** `OutlineSchema` goes out as native structured output. The canonical OUTLINE.md is an 8-column table (`# | slug | title | role | depends_on | word target | assigned_sources | voice`), and `parseOutline` reads that table, the legacy 6-column table and CRLF input.
- **Validation (GRND-08, GRND-10).** `outline-validate.ts` checks unique slugs, known and acyclic `depends_on`, citekeys that exist in LIBRARY.json, a ±20% word budget, at most 2 source-less body sections and the counterargument rule (`counterargument.ts`). A failure gets one corrective turn. If it still fails, the run exits 1 and writes `OUTLINE.rejected.md`, leaving OUTLINE.md, STATE.json and sections byte-identical. Bare runs then report attention and never re-bill.
- **Sections (GRND-09).** Section ids take the form `3` or `1a` (`section-id.ts`, STATE v3), and folders are found by slug. Outline approval writes stub PLAN.md files (plan frontmatter v2). `outline --force` with the `reoutline` gate keeps every kept section byte- and mtime-identical, archives dropped sections to `sections/_archive/` and inserts new ones as `01a-…`. STATE.json is the single authority on section identity (`section-registry.ts`, D-18-38). `pensmith outline` without `--force` applies a hand-edited OUTLINE.md with no model call.
- **Sources (FEED-01..03).** `bin/lib/source-context.ts` is a pure builder, property-tested. The outline receives every source the verifier can check, and the planner and drafter receive only their section's allowed set, fenced. `verifierBlindSpot` withholds entries with no DOI, a DataCite DOI, a synthetic DOI outside a dry run, or a retraction flag (D-18-37, D-18-46). It names them in a WARN and at the approval gate.
- **Planner (GRND-12, GRND-13).** The planner receives the brief, the outline row, upstream claim summaries and its sources. Its output (`SectionPlannerSchema`) is validated: echoed identity, `assigned_sources` ⊆ the allowed set ⊆ LIBRARY, and claim sources ⊆ `assigned_sources`. A failure gets one corrective turn; if it still fails, the run reports `planner output invalid`, writes nothing and leaves PLAN.md unchanged. PLAN.md is rendered with `## Claims`, `## Structure`, `## Word target` and `## Voice`, and `status: planned`. The allowed set is the outline row ∪ the current PLAN.md ∪ `plan-research:§<id>` additions (review round 1).
- **Drafter and containment (FEED-02, FEED-04).** A strict `DrafterInput` holds the full payload, and `buildDrafterRequest` builds the request from it alone, so wave writes and single writes send byte-identical bodies. Voice precedence is PLAN > OUTLINE > STYLE.json > preset tone. `draft-containment.ts` `checkDraft` gets one corrective turn. If the draft still violates, it goes to `DRAFT.rejected.md`, PLAN.md is set to `status: failed` with `failure_reason`, and the run exits 4. Compile, done and verify then refuse the section by name.
- **Citation grammar (D-18-40, D-18-42).** `citation-token.ts` reads every form Pandoc renders as a citation: clusters, locators, `[-@k]`, `@{k}` and narrative `@k`, following Pandoc's key grammar. It skips a citation only where Pandoc provably reads code. This is checked against a recorded corpus of 117 pandoc 3.9 cases (`tests/citation-grammar-pandoc.test.ts`); differential fuzzing found 0 misses in about 40k documents. Containment, Pass 1, GATE-04, Pass-3 attribution, density, revise, the export bibliography and compile's smoother guard all use it. The offline renderer keeps prefixes and locators.
- **write → verify (GRND-15) and wave write (GRND-16).** `write N` and wave write verify each draft unless `--no-verify`. A malformed PLAN.md produces one line naming the file and the field, and independent sections still draft. `DEFAULT_MAX_PARALLEL = 5` matches the docs, and `--max-parallel 1` no longer prints a warning.

### Bare runs, the dry-run workspace and the corpus (workflow stream; GRND-18, GRND-19, D-18-28..31, D-18-39, D-18-41)
- **One step per invocation (GRND-18).** `runNextStep` in `bin/pensmith.ts` is shared by bare runs, `next` and `resume`. One step is plan → write → verify for the next section, or one verb otherwise. It prints `pensmith: ran …; next: …` and exits with the last verb's code. The router never loops a paid call. It reports attention for a failed outline, a failed write, an unchanged failed verification, a blocking UNVERIFIABLE verification, or a divergence between OUTLINE.md and STATE.json. For advisory UNVERIFIABLE rows it moves on (D-18-43). Staleness is decided from content through `.paper/COMPILE-INPUTS.json` (D-18-39).
- **Dry-run workspace (GRND-19).** `--dry-run` works in `./.paper-dry-run/`, seeded from `.paper/` with a `SEED.json` fingerprint that keeps mtimes. The workspace is kept across dry runs and re-seeded when `.paper/` changes. The real `.paper/` is never created or written, no registry entry is made, and exports are named `DRAFT.dry-run.<ext>` and `CITATIONS.dry-run.{bib,ris}`. Bare `pensmith --dry-run --yolo` goes from intake to done in one invocation (D-18-30).
- **The recorded e2e corpus (D-18-31).** `tests/fixtures/e2e-corpus/{MANIFEST,mock-script}.json` plus 18 cassettes under `tests/fixtures/cassettes/e2e/`, recorded with `PENSMITH_CONTACT_EMAIL=pensmith-dev@example.org` (redacted). `npm run cassettes:refresh -- --corpus e2e` re-records it, `tests/helpers/e2e-chain.ts` is the chain sandbox, and HARDEN-01 reuses it.
- **`--help` lists what `--yolo` answers**, generated from `gates.ts` summaries (D-18-41).
